Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Seat Savant operates the Seat Savant Listings site at listings.seatsavant.com. The site is an internal tool for Seat Savant team members. It shows listing, sales and ticket intelligence information. Access is limited to people whom Seat Savant has approved.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, first name, last name and team. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We also record whether your account has been approved.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, to check that you are approved before showing any listing data, and to contact you about your access.

Cookies and browser storage

The site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores your sign-in session in your browser's session storage. This is cleared when you close the tab or sign out.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store accounts and data and to handle sign-in, Google to provide the optional Google sign-in, and Render to host the site. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your access is active. You can ask us to remove your account at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request removal of your account, contact harry@seatsavant.com.
